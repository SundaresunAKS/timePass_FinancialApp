1. The "Starting Line" (Date Filter)
April 1st, 2026: This is the "magic date." The app is programmed to ignore any SMS received before this.

Automatic Scanning: Every time you receive a text from a bank or credit card, the app reads it. If the date is April 1st or later, it saves a copy in the "SMS-uncategorized" section.

2. The Sorting Process (Uncategorized vs. Categorized)
This is the heart of your app’s workflow:

SMS-uncategorized (The Inbox): When a new transaction arrives (via UPI or Credit Card), it lands here first. You will see the full SMS text and the amount.

The Action: You click on a transaction and a menu pops up with your 19 categories (Travel, Fuel, Parumal Box, etc.).

Moving Day: Once you pick a category, that transaction disappears from "Uncategorized" and appears in "SMS-categories."

Mistakes? If you realize you put a "Fuel" expense into "Food," you simply go to the "SMS-categories" tab, click it, and change it.

3. The Dashboard (Your Financial Map)
Pick a Month/Year: At the top of the screen, you choose the month (e.g., May 2026).

The Pie Chart: The app automatically looks at all your "Categorized" items for that month and creates a colorful chart.

Example: If you spent ₹5,000 on Rent and ₹5,000 on Food, the chart will show 50% for each.

Income vs. Expense: The dashboard will also calculate your total "Income" category versus your "Loan" or "Debt" categories to show your balance.

4. Menu & Customization
Txd Category Menu: This is where you manage your list. If you want to add a new category like "Gifts" or edit "Home-Krr," you do it here.

Navigation: You will have a side menu to quickly jump between the Dashboard, your Inbox (Uncategorized), and your History (Categorized).

5. Saving Your Data (The Excel Export)
When you are ready to back up your data or view it on a computer:

You click "Export to Excel."

The app creates a spreadsheet file.

The Columns in Excel will be:

Date: The day you spent the money.

Amount: The exact money value.

Category: Which folder you put it in.

Mode: How you paid (UPI or Credit Card).

Description: The full original text of the SMS (so you never lose the original proof).

Google Drive: If you’ve linked your email, the app will automatically upload this file to your Drive so you can open it on any device.

6. Summary of Your Categories
The app will pre-load these specific names for you:

Personal, Travel, Health/medical, Rent, Home-Krr, Savings, EmergencySavings, Debt, Parumal Box, Mva Acc, ShortLoan, Income, ReturnShortLoan, Food, Fuel, Loan, Others, Savings-ToDad, Savings-PPF.
